# 🎤 SIH Finals: Live Demo Script & Team Guide
**Target Audience:** Evaluators/Judges
**Goal:** Demonstrate the entire Value Chain solution in 5 minutes.

---

## 🕒 The 5-Minute Pitch Flow

### 1️⃣ Introduction (30 Seconds)
*   **Speaker:** [Team Lead]
*   **Screen:** Landing Page (Home)
*   **Action:** Scroll slowly through the Hero section.
*   **Script:** "India imports 60% of its edible oil. Why? Because our value chain is broken. Farmers don't know what to grow, and Processors can't find quality produce. Presenting **Agri-Sync**: India's first AI & Blockchain-powered unified platform for Oilseed Optimization."

---

### 2️⃣ Phase 1: The Farmer Experience (1.5 Minutes)
*   **Speaker:** [Teammate 1]
*   **Screen:** Login as **Farmer** > Dashboard
*   **Key Feature:** AI Advisor & Sowing Planner

**Step-by-Step:**
1.  **Click:** Open **AI Advisor**.
2.  **Action:** Click the chip **"🌾 Crop Health"** or type "Best sowing time for Soybean?".
3.  **Say:** "Unlike basic chatbots, our Context-Aware AI knows I'm a farmer in Wardha. It analyzes real-time weather and soil data to give specific advice."
4.  **Click:** Open **"Sowing Planner"** (Toggle button).
5.  **Action:** Select 'Soybean', 'Wardha', '5 Acres'. Click **Generate**.
6.  **Say:** "This is our USP. We don't just give text; we generate a scientific schedule. See? It calculates yield (10q/acre) and fertilizer needs instantly."

---

### 3️⃣ Phase 2: The Processor & Demand (1.5 Minutes)
*   **Speaker:** [Teammate 2]
*   **Screen:** Switch Role to **Processor** (using Profile dropdown)
*   **Key Feature:** Demand Forecasting & Contracts

**Step-by-Step:**
1.  **Click:** Go to **Dashboard** or **Demand Forecast** page.
2.  **Action:** Show the charts/graphs.
3.  **Say:** "Processors usually fly blind. Here, they use our **Predictive Analytics** to see demand spikes for the upcoming festival season."
4.  **Click:** **AI Advisor** again.
5.  **Action:** Click **"📉 Sourcing"**.
6.  **Say:** "Notice the AI context switch? Now it answers as a corporate buyer, advising on procurement rates and quality specs. Same bot, different brain."

---

### 4️⃣ Phase 3: Traceability & Conclusion (1 Minute)
*   **Speaker:** [Team Lead]
*   **Screen:** Blockchain/Traceability Page (or Retailer View)
*   **Key Feature:** Trust & Blockchain

**Step-by-Step:**
1.  **Click:** **Traceability** Menu.
2.  **Action:** Enter a Batch ID (or show a valid scan).
3.  **Say:** "Finally, for the consumer. This QR scan pulls data from our Blockchain ledger. It proves this oil came from that specific farmer in Wardha, ensuring 100% purity."
4.  **Closing:** "We solve the problem at the root (Farmer), the middle (Processor), and the end (Consumer). Thank you."

---

## ❓ Q&A Cheat Sheet (For Judges' Questions)

**Q: "How is this different from Kisan Call Centers?"**
*   **A:** "Call centers are reactive and manual. Ours is **Proactive and AI-driven**. We also connect the market link (Processors) which call centers don't do."

**Q: "Is the AI hallucinating?"**
*   **A:** "We use RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) grounded on valid agricultural datasets (ICAR/KVK), not just open internet data."

**Q: "How does the blockchain work?"**
*   **A:** "We capture hash data at three points: Harvest, Processing, and Packaging. This creates an immutable chain of custody."

**Q: "Is it scalable?"**
*   **A:** "Yes, the frontend is React (modular), and the backend uses microservices. We can onboard 1 million farmers without performance lag."
