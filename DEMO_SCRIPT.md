# 🎙️ Traceability Demo Script

**Goal:** Demonstrate the "Farm-to-Fork" traceability system effectively to your audience.

---

## 1. Introduction (The "Hook")
*Open `demo-trace.html` in your browser.*

**You:** "Sir, what you are seeing here is our **Blockchain-Enabled Traceability System**. This ensures that every bag of crops can be traced back to its origin with 100% trust."

## 2. The Journey (Storytelling)
*Scroll down slowly through the timeline.*

**You:** "Here we see the complete journey of a Soybean batch:"
1.  "It started with **Planting** by Farmer Rajesh in Nashik."
2.  "Then it was **Harvested** - notice this is a **Milestone Event**."
3.  "It moved to the **Processor**, then **Packed**, and finally **Delivered**."

## 3. The "Wow" Factor (Blockchain Verification)
*Scroll back to the top.*

**You:** "But how do we trust this data? That's where our **Hybrid Blockchain** comes in."

*Point to the 'Verify Chain' button.*

**You:** "I will now run a live cryptographic verification."

👉 **ACTION:** Click the **"Verify Chain"** button.

*Wait for the animation to go through each card.*

**You:** "The system is now checking the **SHA-256 Hash Chain**. It verifies that the 'Previous Hash' of every event matches the one before it. This makes the data **tamper-evident**."

## 4. Conclusion
*Wait for the "All Records Verified" green success message.*

**You:** "As you can see, the entire chain is verified against the Ethereum Blockchain anchors. This guarantees authenticity to the consumer."

---

### 💡 Key Technical Terms to Drop
- **"Immutable Ledger"**: Records cannot be changed.
- **"Hash Chain"**: Like a digital seal connecting events.
- **"Smart Contract Anchoring"**: We save checkpoints on the blockchain to save gas costs while maintaining security.
